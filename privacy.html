Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The short version

PPCF Atlas is designed so that your financial data never leaves your iPhone. We don't run a backend, we don't run ads, we don't require an account, and we don't track you. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Who we are

PPCF Atlas ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance and investment companion distributed on the Apple App Store under the name PPCF Atlas. You can reach us at becipora79@gmail.com.

2. Information we do NOT collect

Because the App is built to work entirely on your device, the following do not happen — by design:

  • We do not create or require an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • We do not collect financial data, transactions, balances, holdings, or receipts.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services that profile users.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data — because we never receive any.

3. Data stored on your device

All financial information you enter — accounts, transactions, receipts, budgets, and portfolio positions — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard storage frameworks. This data lives in the App's sandboxed container and is protected by iOS.

Receipt photos you capture or import are also stored only on your device. If you delete the App, this data is removed with it.

4. iOS system permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs to function, and only at the moment they're needed:

  • Camera — used solely to capture receipt photos at your request. Images are saved on-device.
  • Photo Library — used solely when you explicitly choose to import a receipt image from your library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. The App continues to work without them, with the receipt-capture feature disabled.

5. Apple frameworks and on-device intelligence

The App uses Apple's on-device frameworks (UIKit, Core Graphics, Foundation, etc.) to draw charts, manage storage, and render the interface. No data is transmitted off your device for these functions.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the App does not collect any information from anyone, this commitment is structural rather than procedural.

7. Your rights and choices

Because all data lives on your device, you have full, direct control:

  • View, edit, or delete any record inside the App at any time.
  • Delete the App to permanently remove all locally stored data.
  • Use iOS backup exclusions if you do not want App data included in iCloud or iTunes backups.

8. International users

Regardless of where you live, the same rule applies: your data stays on your device. We do not transfer, store, or process your personal information on any server, in any country, at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the App. Because the App collects nothing, any future change is most likely to be a clarification rather than a new data practice.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact us at:
